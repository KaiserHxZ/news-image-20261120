# The Day's News — Friday, 20 November 2026

One image that sums up the world's news of Friday, 20 November 2026,
drawn entirely in code: a newspaper front page, one day unfolding from
the overnight strikes over Kyiv to the evening earthquake in Peru.

`main.ts` (Node 22+, standard library only, no network) draws the
1024×1024 PNG with its own rasteriser, bitmap font and PNG encoder,
and writes it to `/output/entry.png`. Running it twice produces
byte-identical output.

Stories, each verified against at least two independent reports:

- **Overnight, Kyiv** — Russia's largest strike package of November:
  400+ drones and 110 missiles; three Dnipro bridges shut, power cut,
  and two missiles fell in Poland.
- **Morning, Odisha** — Cyclone Akvan came ashore near Gopalpur with
  80 km/h winds; 300,000 evacuated, heavy rain, no deaths reported.
- **The Hague** — a day after the world court's genocide ruling,
  Myanmar's regime dismissed it as unfair.
- **New York** — an AI sell-off put the S&P 500 and the Dow into
  correction; the Nasdaq fell 5.3% on the day.
- **Evening, Junín, Peru** — a magnitude 6.7 earthquake in the Andes,
  felt in Lima; no deaths reported at first.
